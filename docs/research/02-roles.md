# 02 · AI Engineering Roles in GCCs

<aside>
💡

Seven roles cover almost every GCC AI opening, paying **~10–20 LPA at entry to 90+ LPA for architects**. The best entry door is an adjacent role: Fortune 500 GCCs have 5–6 adjacent roles for every core AI role ([ANSR AI Talent Trends 2025](https://cxotoday.com/press-release/indias-gccs-enter-the-age-of-ai-126000-professionals-now-powering-enterprise-ai-transformation-reveals-new-ansr-report/)). GCCs are 30–35% of India's AI hiring, with ~1 qualified GenAI engineer per 10 open roles ([TeamLease Digital](https://www.thepeoplesboard.com/news/only-1-qualified-engineer-for-every-ten-genai-roles-report/)).

</aside>

### Seniority ladders differ by parent

- **Walmart:** SWE II (P2) → SWE III (P3) → Senior → Staff → Principal → Distinguished
- **Banks (JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs):** Analyst → Associate → Senior Associate → VP → Director
- **Target, Lowe's:** L3/L4 → L5 Senior → L6 Lead

All approximate.
