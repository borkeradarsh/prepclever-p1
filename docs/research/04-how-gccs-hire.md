# 04 · How GCCs Hire & Who Does the Hiring

<aside>
💡

Most GCC tech hiring runs one loop: **OA → 1–2 DSA rounds → design or ML round → hiring manager → HR**, and 54–67% of candidates go from interview to offer in under 2 weeks ([AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/interviews/american-express-interview-questions)). Large captives hire through their own talent teams and referrals; new GCCs are staffed by BOT enablers and GCC-specialist staffing firms — the door for most service-company engineers.

</aside>

## The interview loop

| Stage | What happens | Company examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0. Sourcing** | Referral, LinkedIn/Naukri application, recruiter or agency call, campus drive or hiring challenge | A Target referral led to an HR call within a week ([Jointaro](https://www.jointaro.com/interviews/companies/target/work-experiences/engineer-bengaluru-april-3-2025-4-efcc360c)) |
| **1. Online assessment** | HackerRank/HackerEarth/Codility, 60–90 min, 2–3 DSA problems, sometimes OS/DBMS/SQL MCQs | Walmart: MCQs + 2 DSA in 90 min ([GfG](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/walmart-interview-experience-off-campus-for-sde-2-2024/)); JPMorgan: 2 problems over 3 days, then HireVue |
| **2. DSA / live coding** | 1–2 rounds, LeetCode easy–medium; Goldman Sachs harder (DP, tries, segment trees) | JPMorgan adds a code-review round ([Jointaro](https://www.jointaro.com/interviews/companies/jpmorgan-chase/work-experiences/software-engineer-3-bengaluru-august-23-2025-4-52603787/)) |
| **3. Design** | LLD for SDE-II, HLD for SDE-III+, ML system design for DS/ML roles | JPMorgan SE-II: design a banking & investment app ([AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/interviews/jpmorgan-chase-and-co-dot-interview-questions/software-engineer-ii)) |
| **4. ML / GenAI round** | Classical ML, stats, A/B tests, SQL; since 2025 also RAG and agents | AmEx Agentic AI Engineer (Jun 2026): “walk me through a production RAG you designed”, LangChain vs LangGraph |
| **5. Hiring manager** | Project deep dive, team fit, “why us”, often a Director or VP | AmEx final round with the Director of Engineering |
| **6. HR** | Compensation, notice period | Sometimes merged with round 5 |

## Campus programmes

- **Walmart CodeHers** (women only, with Unstop): MCQ → coding → interview → internship/PPI; ~1 lakh participants and 300+ hires over two editions ([CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/walmart-codehers-indias-biggest-hiring-challenge-for-women-coders-is-back-in-association-with-unstop/)).
- **JPMorgan Code for Good:** CGPA ≥ 8.5, HackerRank → HireVue → hackathon as the final hiring stage ([GfG](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/jp-morgan-chase-co-interview-experience-code-for-good-hackathon-2024/)).
- **Walmart Sparkplug:** 24 pre-selected institutes, CGPA ≥ 7 ([Talentd](https://www.talentd.in/articles/walmart-sparkplug-summer-internship-complete-details-eligibility-stages)).
