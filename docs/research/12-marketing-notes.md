# Marketing

- **Experience:** 56% of GCCs prefer candidates with four to 10 years of experience, while early-career talent makes up 30%.
- **Shortage:** The talent gap reached 43% in AI, data and analytics and 38% in platform engineering. This talent gap creates opportunities for skilled professionals.

Landscape Report puts the GCC pay premium over IT services at 15–22%, stable through 2025-26

Beyond Salaries, Professionals Switch for:

- **Product ownership:** you build and run products rather than delivering to clients
- **Technical career tracks:** you can grow as an architect or principal engineer without moving into people management
- **Predictable hours:** Internal teams typically face less client-billing pressure
- **Global exposure:** collaborate with teams across the US, Europe and Asia.
