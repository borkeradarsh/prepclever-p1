# 01 · GCC Landscape in India

<aside>
💡

India has **2,117 GCCs running 3,728 units**, employing 2.36 million people and earning $98.4B in FY26 ([Nasscom–Zinnov 2026](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/)). GCC hiring is projected at about **5.1 lakh jobs in 2026**, and **64% of new GCC roles need AI, data science or automation skills** ([foundit via Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/at-5-1-lakh-jobs-indias-global-capability-centre-hiring-boom-scales-new-peak-540328-2026-07-01)).

</aside>

## Cities

Six cities (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, NCR, Pune, Mumbai, Chennai) host 92% of GCCs; Bengaluru alone has 880+ centres and Hyderabad 355+ ([Zinnov](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/5-shifts-defining-indias-global-capability-centers-gccs-story-in-2025-blog)). Hyderabad is where most 2025–26 launches landed. Tier-2 cities (Coimbatore, Kochi, Ahmedabad, Indore, Jaipur) are growing fastest from a small base.

### Share of GCC hiring by city, H1 2026

Bengaluru takes 30% of GCC hiring; tier-2 cities now match Hyderabad.

## How to check headcount on LinkedIn

Company page → **People** tab → filter by location (e.g., Hyderabad) and “What they do: Engineering / Data Science”. The associated-members count gives a working headcount; save it with the date checked.
