## Purpose and scope

- **Target reader:** a college student or service-company engineer who wants AI roles at GCCs but does not know how to get there.
- **Scope:** AI roles (AI platform, AI data, GenAI, MLOps), with backend roles only where they feed AI work.
- **Order of segments:** follows the brief — GCC landscape → roles → skill syllabus → who hires → tracks by experience → profile and interview prep → resource links.
- **Yield:** 120+ usable items (17 GCC launches, 7 roles with live postings, 26 hiring firms, 16 careers pages, 52 learning links) backed by 151 unique source links, all listed in the Appendix segment.
