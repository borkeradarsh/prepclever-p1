## Proposed website pages

1. **Home:** “64% of new GCC roles need AI skills”, with an entry point per track (fresher / switcher / mid / senior).
2. **GCC directory:** company, city, launch date, AI focus and LinkedIn headcount, filterable by city (segment 01).
3. **Role pages:** title variants, ladder, pay band, live postings (segment 02).
4. **Syllabus per role:** checklists linked to the resource library (segments 03 and 07).
5. **Roadmap by experience:** a 3–6 month plan per track (segment 05).
6. **How GCCs hire:** interview loop, BOT enablers and recruiters, careers pages (segment 04).
7. **Profile toolkit:** resume template, GitHub checklist, LinkedIn setup, interview prep (segment 06).
8. **Resource library:** filtered YouTube, course, repo and sample-resume links (segment 07).
