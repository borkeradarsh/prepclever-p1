## 🎓 Fresher (0–1 yr)

- **Target titles:** SWE I/II, Associate Engineer, Analyst (banks), Analyst – Data Science, Data Engineer I
- **Realistic entry route:** campus programmes (Walmart Sparkplug, JPMorgan Code for Good, Amex and Lowe's DS drives); else off-campus into SWE/DE, then an internal move to AI. ~6 in 10 fresher postings want internships.
- **3–6 month plan:** ~150 LeetCode + SQL; scikit-learn basics; one deployed RAG app (FastAPI + vector DB + eval set); one Spark/Databricks pipeline; an internship.
- **Pay to expect (LPA):** GCC entry 15–25 (Walmart P2 20, JPMorgan Analyst 18, Amex 25, Lowe's L3 15) vs TCS ~7, Infosys ~5.5

## 🔁 Service switcher (2–5 yrs)

- **Target titles:** SWE II/III, Sr AI Engineer, Associate (banks), AI Data Engineer II, MLOps Engineer
- **Realistic entry route:** lateral via Naukri, Instahyre, LinkedIn and referrals; enter through the adjacent role you already do and add the AI layer.
- **3–6 month plan:**
    - **Months 1–2:** Python, FastAPI, SQL, DSA
    - **Months 2–4:** production RAG + LangGraph agent with evals, tracing, guardrails on Docker/K8s
    - **Months 4–6:** MLflow + Airflow or Databricks embedding pipeline; one cloud certification
- **Pay to expect (LPA):** from Infosys/TCS/Wipro 9–18 to GCC SWE II/III 28–34 — roughly 1.5–2.5x

## 📈 Mid (5–10 yrs)

- **Target titles:** Senior/Staff MLE, Applied AI ML Sr Associate or Lead, Lead AI Engineer, Staff DS
- **Realistic entry route:** lateral at Senior/Staff; system design and project deep dives decide the level.
- **3–6 month plan:** ML/LLM system design (serving, latency, cost, evals), multi-agent orchestration, model-risk controls, Kubernetes/Ray, one domain; open-source one GenAI component.
- **Pay to expect (LPA):** 35–90 — Walmart Senior 53 / Staff 91; JPMorgan VP 63; Goldman VP 67; Target L6 79

## 🏛️ Senior (10+ yrs)

- **Target titles:** Principal SWE – AIML, Technical Lead Architect, Applied AI ML Director, ML Platform Architect
- **Realistic entry route:** networks and executive search; new GCC AI CoE build-outs (250+ AI CoEs).
- **3–6 month plan:** enterprise AI platform architecture, agentic architecture (A2A, MCP, Graph-RAG), AI governance, LLM FinOps, team building.
- **Pay to expect (LPA):** 75–200 — ML Platform Architect ~95 at 15 yrs; Walmart Principal 126; Nike E-band 101

*Pay sources: TeamLease, [Levels.fyi](http://Levels.fyi) (self-reported total comp), Zyoin, Taggd — all approximate.*
